version: 2026-10-03k
<b>Projectiles</b>
- Projectiles fired together now hit each target only once: Ice Shard's fan, extra arrows, extra Fire Bolts (and their bursts). The first one to reach an enemy hurts it, the rest pass it by. Spread shots are for crowds now, not for stacking on one enemy.
- Ice Shard's shards hit harder to make up for it.
- Monster archers' three-arrow volleys hurt you once at most.
<b>Items</b>
- An item never rolls a stat it already has: no extra Armour on a plate helm, no second life line on a belt.
<b>Spells nerfed hard</b>
- Spell damage is much lower: base damage cut by about 40%, and each skill level adds 35% instead of 75% (a level 10 spell does about 4x a level 1, not 7.75x).
- Intelligence adds 0.6% spell damage per point (was 1.2%). Spell Damage on staves and jewellery is roughly halved.
- Fire Bolt's burst is smaller and splashes for less; Chain Lightning loses more damage with each arc. Staff spells cast a little slower.
<b>World</b>
- The charred woods round the Ashen Ruins (and the dead woods of the Graveyard) are much thicker, with rubble between the trees, so it's clear you can't walk through.
<b>Bosses are deadlier</b>
- Bosses move as fast as you (Rimeheart faster), hit about 40% harder and attack about 50% faster.
- Their big moves come more often and wind up much faster, cover more ground, and aim where you're heading: leaps land past where you're running, the Warlord's fissure follows your path, and falling fire and ice lead you.
