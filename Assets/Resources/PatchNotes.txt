version: 2026-10-02b
<b>Fixes</b>
- Casting Chain Lightning or Fire Bolt with a bow no longer also shoots an arrow (and with a melee weapon no longer adds a free swing).
- Clicking an item to pick it up no longer swings at the air on the same click.
- Clicking a townsperson or a waystone is more forgiving (their name tag counts too), and never shoots them.
- Clicking a gate walks you through it.
- Haven is listed first on the waystone.
- Right-clicking in the game no longer opens the browser menu in Firefox.
- Bows re-aim at the target when the arrow leaves, so shooting while strafing hits what you clicked. Arrows also hit enemies they visibly graze.

<b>Loot</b>
- Each rarity has its own drop sound; uniques ring out. (The odd thud when an enemy died was the old item-drop sound.)
- Picking up and putting down items sounds like what they are: weapons clink, armour thumps, jewellery glimmers.
- Item base stats now vary a little (a 15 evasion base rolls 12 to 16).
- Items dropping on the same spot spread out so every icon shows, and items and their names draw over corpses.
- Hover an item on the ground to see its stats; hovering items while holding one shows their stats too.
- Bows and quivers drop much more often, and there's a new early Crude Bow.
- More caster and archer gear: Silk Robe, Silk Gloves, Silk Slippers, Sage Circlet, Hunter Hood, Mystic Robe; Intelligence and Dexterity roll on more slots.

<b>Balance</b>
- Spells gain 1.5% damage per Intelligence (was 2%).
- Chain Lightning reaches 9 m (was 12) and rewards aim: cast on an enemy under the cursor for full damage (+25% up close); a blind cast hits for 70%. Each arc deals 70% of the one before.

<b>Interface</b>
- A pulsing note above the skill bar shows when you have passive points to spend (click it or press P).
- The chat sits under the inventory and its tooltips. When you're not typing its background disappears and messages fade after a while; scroll the mouse wheel over it to read back.
- A soft white light around your character keeps nearby things readable in the dark.
- This window: a note of what changed, once per update.
