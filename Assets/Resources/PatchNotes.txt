<b>Spectators</b>
- The chat now always draws over everything else for spectators (skill tree, inventory, every other window), and Enter always brings you back to the chat box.
- New buttons over the chat (Bag, Character, Tree, Skills) open the player's menus for you, whatever the player has open.
- You can close a menu the player opened - just for yourself; it stays open for them, and shows again the next time they open it.
<b>First summoner skill: Raise Skeletons</b>
- A new bar skill raises two skeleton warriors that follow you and attack enemies nearby for about half a minute (at most 2 at a time, one more at levels 4, 7 and 10). They hit harder with the skill's level, Intelligence and Spell Damage.
- It rolls on helmets, amulets and staves, and the starting Iron Helmet carries it at level 1.
- Early version: enemies don't fight back against the skeletons yet, and spectators can't see them yet.
