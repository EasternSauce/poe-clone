version: 2026-10-03r
<b>The Summoner</b>
- Minions now fight for real: enemies attack a minion that is nearer than you (and always a Bone Golem close to them), and minions have life of their own (a green bar when hurt).
- Minions don't use your own damage, life or Intelligence. Their life and damage come from the summon's level (steeply, level by level) and from new minion stats: Minion Life, Minion Damage, Minion Speed, Minions take less damage. Without investing in them they fall in a few hits - a summoner who wants an army that holds the line has to build for it.
- Summon levels: new "+1 to Level of all Summon Skills" and "+1/+2 to Level of Raise Skeletons / Skeleton Mages / Spirit Wolves / Bone Golem" stats on sceptres, grimoires, helmets, gloves, boots, body armour, amulets and rings. "+1 to all Spells" doesn't raise summons.
- Raise Skeletons: skeleton warriors now stay until destroyed. At most 2 (one more at levels 7 and 12); casting again at the cap replaces the most battered one.
- New summons: Skeleton Mages (hang back and cast bolts, frail), Spirit Wolves (a fast pack for 12 seconds that enemies can't touch) and Bone Golem (a big temporary brute that taunts enemies round it).
- New one-handed weapon: the Sceptre. Its blows put Death Mark on what they hit. A Bone Sceptre carrying Raise Skeletons now lies on the benches in Haven at the start (Raise Skeletons is no longer on the starting helmet).
- New off-hand: the Grimoire, for a one-handed weapon or empty hand. Its attack is Death Mark, a bolt that marks an enemy: your minions rush it and hit it 30% harder.
- Grave Rot, a curse that rolls on grimoires (and now and then helmets): enemies where you aim deal 20% less damage and take 15% more for 6 seconds, more with each level.
- A Necromancy cluster on the passive tree, out past Fire Ward between Wisdom and Zeal: minion life and damage, Lord of Bones (+1 to all summons) and four keystones - Bone Legion, Soul Bond, Death's Herald, Spirit Pact.
- Two new uniques: The Ossuary Codex (grimoire) and Gravewarden's Rod (sceptre).
- Spectators see every minion, with green bars.
<b>Other</b>
- Teleport rolls on gear twice as often.
- Fixed area skills (Frost Nova, Cleave, Chain Lightning...) and melee blows sometimes missing enemies in cluttered places like Haven's plaza.
