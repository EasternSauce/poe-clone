version: 2026-10-06-travel-passive-reset
- Travel passives now grant either +3 to a nearby attribute or a small bonus suited to their branch, bridge, or destination. Longer paths keep the same bonus throughout.
- Poison skill grants on items now show their level in the same format as other skills.
- Maces and sceptres lift overhead, then swing down on basic attacks.
- Two-handed basic attacks no longer draw an area circle on the ground.
- Picking up equipment from the ground or inventory now uses the soft armour sound; rings and amulets keep their jewellery sound.
- Potion pickup sounds play at half their previous volume.
- Existing characters receive a one-time passive tree reset with all points returned and respec charges preserved.
- Escape now pauses the game while the settings menu is open; closing it resumes play.
