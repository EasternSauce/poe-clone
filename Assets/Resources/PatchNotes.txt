version: 2026-10-03i
<b>Skills now come from gear</b>
- You no longer learn skills by levelling. Every skill is found on gear instead, at a level from 1 to 10, and a higher level hits harder, costs a little more and comes back sooner.
- New weapon: staves (two-handed). Every staff carries a spell that becomes your attack: left click (or the aim stick) casts it instead of swinging. Out of mana, you swing the staff instead.
- Staff spells: Fire Bolt, Chain Lightning and the new Ice Shard, which flings three chilling shards in a narrow fan.
- Staves roll caster stats: spell damage, cast speed, faster cooldowns, mana, mana regeneration, area of effect, "+1 to level of spells", and now and then a second skill.
- Other skills roll where they fit: Cleave on melee weapons, Dash on boots, Frost Nova on helmets and gloves, Rejuvenate on amulets and belts.
- New stats: "+1 to Level of all Spells" on amulets, "+1 to Level of all Fire/Cold/Lightning Spells" on rings and amulets, Cast Speed and faster Skill Cooldowns on jewellery and other gear.
- Tougher monsters in later areas drop higher-level skills.
- A Gnarled Staff with Fire Bolt now lies on the benches in Haven, and the starting boots carry Dash.
- Characters from before this change find a staff with Fire Bolt in their bag.
<b>Controls</b>
- The skill bar has eight slots: Q, E, R, F, and the right, middle and two side mouse buttons. The square on its left shows your attack.
- The skills panel (K) shows each skill's level and which item it comes from.
- Bow shots, spells and melee swings now go wherever you are aiming at the moment they leave your hand, not where you aimed when you started.
<b>Balance</b>
- Life and mana regenerate more slowly.
- Enraged monsters are much faster, attack 50% faster, and lead their shots and aimed spells to where you are heading.
- A monster you kill from a distance no longer flashes "ENRAGED" as it dies.
<b>Gear</b>
- A Short Bow now lies on the benches in Haven with the rest of the starting gear.
- Cleave needs a melee weapon: it can no longer be used with a bow.
- New stat: Melee Range, found on gloves and weapons and on the Brute Force passive.
- Area of Effect can now roll on amulets and helmets, and the Arcane Mind passive grants it.
<b>Bosses</b>
- A slain boss returns after two minutes, but only once you are well away from its lair.
<b>Interface</b>
- The inventory, stash/trader and character windows have an X button to close them.
- The character page now shows above the level/life/mana panel instead of under it.
<b>Monsters</b>
- New creatures roam the wilds: giant spiders, dire wolf packs and bog slimes in Greenwood; grave bat swarms, corpse oozes and crypt spiders in the Graveyard; magma beetles and hellhound packs in the Ashen Ruins; frost wolves and ice crawlers in the Frozen Hollow.
- Slimes and oozes burst into smaller ones when killed.
- Spiders, wolves and slimes leap at you, aiming where you are heading: a glow on the ground shows where they will land, and anyone in their path is hit too.
- Every creature has its own sounds, movement and death.
- Humanoid monsters (and you) now breathe while standing still and put their whole body into each swing.
- Monsters move faster, and keep chasing you much further.
- Hit a monster from a distance and it may become enraged for a few seconds: much faster, hitting harder and attacking quicker.
- Small creatures like slimelings can now be hit by arrows, spells and melee.
- The minimap spots monsters further away, even in ground you haven't explored yet.
<b>World</b>
- Removed the gate in the Ashen Ruins that led straight back to Haven; use the town portal or a waystone to go home.
