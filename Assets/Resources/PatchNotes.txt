version: 2026-10-03d
<b>Fixes</b>
- Menus (inventory, character page, stash/trading) no longer let a click through to an NPC standing behind them.
- Clicking an NPC now requires already being reasonably close to them, so an accidental click on a distant/background NPC won't send you walking across the map.
- Performance: only your current area simulates its enemies now (other areas used to keep running in the background the whole time); mobile rendering was also tuned down (antialiasing, shadow quality), which should help frame rate noticeably, especially on phones.
