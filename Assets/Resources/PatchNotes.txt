<b>Spectating</b>
- Spectators now see the player's inventory, stash/trader, character page, passive tree and skills panel when the player opens them, and the stats of whatever item the player points at. Move your own mouse to look around yourself; after a moment it goes back to following the player. Spectators can also open these (I, C, P, K) on their own.
<b>World</b>
- Rivers (and lava/frozen rivers) now have raised banks instead of floating flat on the grass, so it's clear you can't walk into them; rivers also flow.
