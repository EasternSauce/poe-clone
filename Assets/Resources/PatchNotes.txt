<b>Mana</b>
- Spells cost far more mana as they level up (+40% per skill level): casting nonstop now empties your mana in about ten seconds unless you have mana regeneration gear. Bring mana potions.
<b>Updates</b>
- When a new version of the game goes live, the page saves your character and reloads onto it by itself within about a minute.
<b>Spectators</b>
- Bolts, shards and arrows now stop and burst on the monsters they hit, instead of flying straight through them.
<b>Items</b>
- Items from older versions are brought up to date when you log in: a stat that can no longer appear on that item is removed, values outside today's range are brought into it, a doubled stat keeps one line, and uniques get their current stats.
- Item levels top out at 20 (traders stocked better than anything that drops for high-level characters).
<b>Fixes</b>
- Clicking the stash or a trader no longer picks up whatever item the window opens under your cursor.
