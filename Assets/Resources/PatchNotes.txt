version: 2026-10-03j
<b>Loot</b>
- Monsters drop gear about half as often (bosses still always drop theirs). Gold and potions are unchanged.
<b>Spell balance</b>
- Spells cost more mana (and 15% more per skill level), recover a little slower and hit a little less hard, especially at high levels. Intelligence adds a bit less spell damage.
- Mana regenerates more slowly from a large mana pool. Casting nonstop now drains your mana: bring mana potions.
<b>Spectators</b>
- Spectators now see your spells: staff bolts and shards, Chain Lightning, Frost Nova, Cleave, Dash and Rejuvenate.
<b>The bosses, reworked</b>
- Gravelord Mortis now wields a huge executioner's axe: he leaps across the crypt and buries it where you stand, whirls it all round himself, and raises the dead.
- The Ashen Warlord swings a two-handed maul: he slams it down and the ground cracks open towards you in a line of fire, quake-leaps onto you, and rains fire.
- Rimeheart is now a giant frost spider, queen of the ice crawlers: she pounces, bursts frost all round her (it chills), hatches her brood, and calls down ice.
- Every big move glows on the ground before it lands. At half life each boss roars into a second phase and attacks faster.
- Heavy blows now shake the screen.
<b>Two-handed weapons</b>
- New weapons: greatswords, greataxes and mauls. They take both hands (no shield), swing slower, but hit far harder, reach further and sweep a wider arc.
- The greatsword sweeps wide from side to side, the greataxe chops down from overhead, and the maul is hauled over the head and slammed into the ground with a jolt.
- Equipping a two-handed weapon (or a bow) puts your shield back in your bag if there's room.
<b>More item variety</b>
- Every kind of gear now has lines for Strength (heavy plate and gauntlets), Dexterity (leather and hoods) and Intelligence (silk robes, circlets, sashes), each with four tiers.
- Deeper areas and tougher monsters drop the newer tiers much more often; the early ones fade out as you go.
- Gear made for a kind of character rolls that kind's stats more often: a silk robe tends to roll mana and Intelligence, plate rolls Strength and Armour.
- New bases: bucklers, kite shields, leather belts, cloth sashes, moonstone rings (mana), amber amulets (Strength), and higher tiers of every ring and amulet.
- New random stats: life regeneration on armour and jewellery, life leech on melee weapons, gloves and rings, and life on kill on attack weapons, gloves, rings and quivers.
