version: 2026-10-06-new-uniques
- Added ten ordinary uniques, including restrained versions of Shatter, Stormblade, Glacial Step, Second Wind, and Deaths Herald. The Last Draught grants Onslaught when you drink a health potion; several existing uniques gained an uncommon effect with lower base stats.

- Dash keeps its 3.5-second cooldown at level 1 and scales to 2.2 uses per second at level 10, before Cooldown Recovery.
