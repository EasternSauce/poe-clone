version: 2026-10-11-melee-skill-toggles
- Melee skills now work like bow skills: press a melee skill on the bar to toggle it on, and while it's on, your weapon attack (left click / the aim stick) uses that skill instead of a plain swing. Only one bow or melee skill can be on at a time. Melee skills no longer have cooldowns. Each use takes about as long as one of your weapon's swings (sped up by attack speed; heavier skills are a bit slower) and costs a little mana (a bit more at higher character levels). Faster weapons pay less per use and slower ones more, so the cost per second is about the same with any weapon; attack speed raises it. Without the mana you swing normally until it's back.
- Melee skill damage per hit is lower now that you can use them back to back, but they still hit harder than a plain swing: Cleave 110% (+4.5% per level), Pulverize 175% (+8%), Reaping Arc 140% (+6%), Lunging Thrust 165% (+7%), Fang Strike 90% (+4%) plus poison worth 95% (+7%) over 3 seconds. Their hits are melee attacks, so critical strikes, leech and attack speed apply.
- Lunging Thrust now lunges only as far as the enemy you're aiming at (up to a few steps) instead of always the full distance.
- Whirlwind is now a toggle too: turn it on from the bar, then hold the attack button to spin towards the cursor. Its mana cost is unchanged, but each hit now deals 32% weapon damage (+1.8% per level), down from 50% (+2.8%): it was far ahead of every other skill.
- New melee skills: Fissure (maces and mauls) splits the ground in a crack that races ahead of you, hitting everything along it. Aftershock (maces and mauls) sends 3 tremors (4 from level 6) rolling ahead, each further out and wider than the last. Cross Slash (daggers) carves an X around you with two quick crossing cuts. Venom Slash (daggers) is a quick, wide slash that poisons everything in front of you.
- Melee skills now roll only on weapons that suit them: Cleave and Whirlwind on swords and axes, Pulverize, Fissure and Aftershock on maces and mauls, and Fang Strike, Cross Slash and Venom Slash on daggers. Daggers roll their skills more often. A mace, maul or dagger you already own loses Cleave or Whirlwind, since those no longer roll on it.
- Melee skills reach big enemies at the edge of their body, like a plain swing, instead of needing to reach their centre (daggers could not reach the larger bosses).

version: 2026-10-11-whirlwind
