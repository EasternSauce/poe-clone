<b>Phones</b>
- Attacking and casting now use a second stick (where the attack button used to be) instead of a button: drag it to aim, hold it off centre to keep attacking or casting that way. Works the same for melee, bows and spells - there's no more auto-aim, so you're always in control of the direction.

<b>Fixes</b>
- Gold dropping on the ground is much quieter.
- Items on the ground are easier to click or tap - the target around them is bigger than the icon itself.
