<b>The Shepherd</b>
- His first-phase charge is much wider and travels farther.
- The main quest now opens the Shed Sanctuary: defeat the Shepherd and his final form, Carrion Saint.
- His colossal serpent pursues you and leaves a dangerous body along its route. Bait it carefully to keep an escape path.
- The act boss drops one of two exclusive poison weapons: Shepherd's Fang (dagger) or Widow's Choir (bow).
- The act boss returns after five minutes. Rare Shed Hearts drop from Act I enemies; click one in your bag to reawaken a defeated boss immediately.
<b>Waystones reset</b>
- Waystone travel has been reset once for every character: visit the areas again to unlock their waystones. Levels, gear, passives and quests are kept.
