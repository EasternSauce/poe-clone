version: 2026-10-05-archer-snake-dash
<b>Final boss polish</b>
- The large serpent now emerges behind Carrion Saint, turns for a one-second warning, then pursues. Its head uses the visible jaw bounds to stay above the floor, with smaller jaw hinges and a head shake while swallowing.
- Carrion Saint closes to bite range before choosing a melee attack.
- Carrion Saint's bite now matches its animation reach. The pursuing serpent weaves, stays clear of the floor, opens its jaws, and tips its head back to swallow; its 20-second cooldown begins after the attack ends.
- Poison globs leave the snake mouths. Hits on the pursuing head show the damage dealt to the boss over that head. The third-phase body carries visible living snakes between attacks.
- Carrion Saint's hind legs buckle on death with a heavy impact and dust. Ordinary corpses last twice as long, and boss corpses four times as long.

<b>Archer and boss balance</b>
- Bow arrows, including skill arrows, travel 50% farther. PC Dash follows the cursor, and phase-three arena zoom stays pulled back until you leave.
- The final boss's pursuing serpent head now has a hitbox and shares half of each hit with the boss.

<b>Boss pacing</b>
- Halved attack tempo for the act bosses, including The Shepherd and Carrion Saint.

<b>Character selection</b>
- Character rows now show portraits of the saved character model wearing its equipped gear.
- Existing single-character saves are recovered into the character list, including saves already copied by the first multi-character release.
<b>Combat balance</b>
- Cleave, Pulverize, Reaping Arc, Lunging Thrust, and Fang Strike each deal 20% more damage at every skill level; Fang Strike's poison damage also rises 20%.
- Dedicated Grace bow passives now grant up to 20% more bow damage, applying to basic shots and all bow skills. Deadeye and Volley grant 5% each; new True Shaft and Longshot passives add another 5% each deeper in the branch.
<b>Melee skills</b>
- Cleave now roots you through a short windup and sweeping strike. Added Pulverize for maces and mauls, Reaping Arc for axes, and Lunging Thrust for swords; each has its own procedural attack pose, weapon-family drop pool, and swing cue at impact. Lunging Thrust drives the character forward before its hit, and Fang Strike stays within dagger reach. Heavy hammer impacts carry a ground burst and camera jolt.
<b>Summoning</b>
- Minions now take full enemy hits through their own armour and fire, cold, lightning, or poison resistance. Their skill details show these defences. Summon life starts higher but grows 5% per level instead of 18%; an early Grave Ward passive and stronger Grave Whispers/Ossuary defences reward summoner investment. Minion damage is unchanged.
- Low-level summon mana costs are reduced so a new summoner can replace fallen minions more readily; the discount fades by summon skill level 10.
- All summon skills now share one global army limit, increased by Necromancy passives and ordinary gear; fully rolled ordinary capacity reaches about seven minions. The Ossuary Codex raises that to about eight and Book of Shed Skin to about nine, with final 20% and 30% less minion damage multipliers applied after outgoing hit modifiers. Specific skeleton capacity is unique-only. Summon mana costs are 2.5 times higher. Minion base damage values are halved, Intelligence adds 0.5% minion damage per point, and the actual Minion Damage values on passives and gear are reduced by about 40%.
<b>Controls</b>
- Skills can now use 1, 2, 3, and 4 as well as Q/E/R/F and mouse buttons. The bottom bar shows those number slots beside Q/E/R/F. Health and mana potion keys are selected in the skills menu; they still default to 1 and 2. Each key holds one skill or potion.
- Unassigned spare mouse buttons no longer appear on the bottom skill bar.
<b>Settings and history</b>
- Chat is off by default for new settings. The Settings page has one working Close button. Patch history has separate scrolling for releases and their notes.
- ESC now opens Settings and the complete patch history. Recovered 24 earlier releases from repository history; future releases are also saved to this device.
- Chat visibility can be turned on in Settings.
<b>UI and boss pacing</b>
- Bosses now have armor and elemental resistance. Field bosses have 500 armor and 20% fire, cold and lightning resistance; the Shepherd has 1200 armor and 30% resistances. The Shepherd and Carrion Saint move, attack, and hit twice as hard, and the Shepherd has four times its previous life. Burrow Snatch hits much harder; phase transition timing is unchanged.
- Stash tabs now have a visible Rename button; editing selects the current name for quick replacement.
- Shed Heart drops now come from level 7+ areas.
- Shed Heart now has its own heart icon instead of borrowing a ring icon.
- Item tooltips now stay readable above chat for players and spectators.
- Choose an existing character or create a named character at startup. Each character keeps separate progress, gear, quests and map discovery.
- Minion health bars no longer overlay the spectator inventory and stash. Removed the permanent Emberwatch ground label and increased the act boss respawn timer to ten minutes.
<b>Quest props and summons</b>
- Fixed quest body clicks being stolen by NPC hit boxes that balloon when their labels cross the camera plane. Removing or replacing a summoning sceptre dismisses its summoned minions.
<b>Combat</b>
- Added fire, cold, lightning, elemental and armour penetration to magic affixes, themed uniques and connected passive-tree paths. Penetration lowers the matching defense; elemental resistance below zero increases damage taken.
- Fire, cold and lightning resistance cap at 75%. Armour now keeps scaling with diminishing returns, so very high physical damage reduction takes a large armour investment.
- Summoning bosses now raise fewer enemies before enraging; their enraged phase can build a larger pack. The spider queen's enraged cap is three crawlers.
- Death interrupts skills still in their animation or channel.
- Axe and hammer strikes now lead with the weapon head. Changing either hand weapon dismisses your minions.
- Final boss phase changes, growth, reveal, and attacks, including phase-two snake strikes, now replicate to spectators.


<b>Poison builds</b>
- Poison is now a damage type. Poison resistance mitigates poison damage and damage over time; poison penetration lowers enemy poison resistance.
- Added poison damage and damage-over-time affixes, plus connected Grace-tree poison branches and Viper's Kiss.
- Added Fang Strike, Venom Arrow, Venom Spout and Summon Viper to gear skill drops. The Viper is a temporary minion whose venom scales with minion and poison investment.
- The Shepherd now rewards one of five exclusive poison uniques, including skill-granting dagger, bow, sceptre and grimoire rewards.
