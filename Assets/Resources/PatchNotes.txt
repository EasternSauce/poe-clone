version: 2026-10-03h
<b>Balance</b>
- Leveling is twice as slow: each level now needs twice the experience.
<b>Minimap</b>
- The minimap no longer gives away gates, the waystone or townsfolk you haven't discovered yet.
- Monsters only show on the minimap when they are close by.
<b>Gear and skills</b>
- A Short Bow now lies on the benches in Haven with the rest of the starting gear.
- Cleave needs a melee weapon: it can no longer be used with a bow.
- New stat: Melee Range, found on gloves and weapons and on the Brute Force passive.
- Area of Effect can now roll on amulets and helmets, and the Arcane Mind passive grants it.
<b>Bosses</b>
- A slain boss returns after two minutes, but only once you are well away from its lair.
<b>Interface</b>
- The inventory, stash/trader and character windows have an X button to close them.
<b>Monsters</b>
- New creatures roam the wilds: giant spiders, dire wolf packs and bog slimes in Greenwood; grave bat swarms, corpse oozes and crypt spiders in the Graveyard; magma beetles and hellhound packs in the Ashen Ruins; frost wolves and ice crawlers in the Frozen Hollow.
- Slimes and oozes burst into smaller ones when killed.
- Spiders, wolves and slimes leap at you, aiming where you are heading: a glow on the ground shows where they will land, and anyone in their path is hit too.
- Every creature has its own sounds, movement and death.
- Humanoid monsters (and you) now breathe while standing still and put their whole body into each swing.
- Monsters move faster, and keep chasing you much further.
- Hit a monster from a distance and it may become enraged for a few seconds: much faster, hitting harder and attacking quicker.
- Small creatures like slimelings can now be hit by arrows, spells and melee.
- The minimap spots monsters further away, even in ground you haven't explored yet.
<b>World</b>
- Removed the gate in the Ashen Ruins that led straight back to Haven; use the town portal or a waystone to go home.
