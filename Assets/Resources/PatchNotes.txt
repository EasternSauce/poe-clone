version: 2026-10-06-armour-and-reconnect
- Armour now halves hits with half as much armour. Player stagger chance scales with life actually lost: hits under 5% of maximum life do not stagger, hits at 35% always stagger before Avoid Stun, and smaller hits scale between those points.
- Once gameplay has started, losing the game server connection no longer pauses the local game or covers it with a reconnect screen. Chat reports disconnects and reconnects; spectator streams catch up when the connection returns.
